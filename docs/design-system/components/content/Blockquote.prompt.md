**Blockquote** — the museum-voice pull quote: serif italic, seal-red opening tick, optional uppercase attribution.

```jsx
<Blockquote cite="Edwyn Chen">
  Good design is the bridge between culture and clarity.
</Blockquote>
<Blockquote tone="invert" size="lg">The Art of Balance.</Blockquote>
```

`tone="invert"` for navy sections. `size`: sm | md | lg.
